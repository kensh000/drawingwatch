A Message from the Developer

Hello! I'm Kensho, the developer of Drawing Watch.

"Drawing Watch" is the first app I've developed with the intention of releasing it to the public.
The idea behind this app came from a rather unusual motivation: I wanted to take on elementary school kids who draw with their fingers on smartphones.

One day, while browsing Twitter, I came across a drawing of a person.
"What an amazing illustration! I have to follow this artist!" I thought. But when I checked their profile... I saw the words "elementary school student" and "finger drawing on a smartphone."
Even now, I still can't find the words to describe the shock I felt at that moment.
Back then, I couldn't even draw people myself, which probably made the shock all the greater.

Although I couldn't draw people, I was able to draw cityscapes and landscapes, so I was part of an illustration community. When I brought this up in that community, someone simply replied:
"Oh, you mean those kids who draw with their fingers on smartphones. Yeah, there are quite a few of them. (*In Japan)"

Drawing people with nothing but their fingers on a tiny smartphone screen. And they're elementary school students, too.
Then, I understood that I'm really nothing special -just an ordinary person.

But I didn't give up.
To give up is to admit defeat.

Was there any way I could possibly compete with these prodigies?

After thinking about it for several years, I finally arrived at an answer.

"I just need to beat them with hardware."

Yeah. The world is surprisingly simple.
There's absolutely no way I can win when it comes to age.
But what if I used even smaller hardware?
This. This is THE LAST WAY against elementary school kids who draw with their fingers on smartphones.
Of course, I'd still need to produce artwork of a comparable quality. But that shouldn't be a problem with enough practice.

I started looking for a device smaller than a smartphone, yet equipped with a touchscreen of comparable quality.
And then I noticed something.
Wait a minute... I already have an AppleWatch right here on my wrist!

Why not just draw on that?
That was my idea.

However, there were few drawing apps for AppleWatch, and none of them quite met my expectations.
Then why not make one myself? And that's how Drawing Watch was born.

I had four goals in developing Drawing Watch:
1.To provide an exceptionally comfortable drawing experience that never gets in the way of your creative flow.
2.To allow finished artwork to be exported at a sufficiently high resolution.
3.To make it possible to create artwork of outstanding quality -even good enough for use in Doujinshi.
4.To be able to compete with elementary school kids who draw with their fingers on smarphones.

Development continued, and then came August 2026.
The journey was anything but smooth. I accidentaly deleted all my artwork data at one point, and my development Mac was left with just 0.6GB of storage space, among other mishaps.

Nevertheless, I managed to submit a artwork created with Drawing Watch to a collaborative Doujinshi I was participating in .
What started as a completely outlandish idea had finally become something real: an app that was genuinely useful.

And soon, Drawing Watch will be available for everyone to use.
