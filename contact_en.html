Contact

If you have any questions, suggestions, or bug reports regarding the app, please contact me using the information below.

What You Can Contact Me About

  • Questions about how to use the app
  • Bug reports and issues with app functionality
  • Feedback and feature requests
  • Feedback or questions regarding the Privacy Policy
  • Artwork submissions for the Example page

Please do not send abusive, offensive, or otherwise inappropriate inquiries.

I may not respond to inquiries that do not fall into any of the categories listed above, or that I consider to be abusive, inappropriate, or otherwise not requiring a response.

Information to Include in Your Inquiry

Please provide the following information whenever possible:

  • The version of Drawing Watch installed on your iPhone (Drawing Watch > About > Version)
  • A description of your inquiry
  • An email adress where I can reach you
  • If your inquiry concerns a bug or you are submitting artwork to the Example page, please also provide the additional information listed below.
For Bug Reports

In addition to the information listed above, please provide the following details whenever possible:

  • The device you are using (Settings App > General > About)
  • Your iOS and watchOS versions (Settings App > General > About)
  • A description of the issue and any error messages displayed
  • The steps you took leading up to the issue
For Artwork Submissions

If you would like to submit your artwork for inclusion in the Example page, please provide the following additional information whenever possible:

  • A note indicating that you are submitting artwork for the Example page
  • A PNG image of your artwork (Please do not send JSON files.)
  • The date the artwork was completed (optional)
  • A comment about your artwork (optional)

Please keep the following points in mind when submitting artwork

  • Submitting artwork does not guarantee that it will be featured in the Example page.
  • When publishing your artwork on this website, I may convert the image to WebP or another format to reduce file size. This conversion may result in a slight loss of image quality compared to the original.
  • I reserve the right to datermine how long submitted artwork remains featured on this website.

Please provideas much detail as possible when contacting me.

Providing detailed information may help me investigate and resolve your issue more quickly.

For information about how your email adress will be handled, please refer to Privacy Policy .

Please note:
  • Depending on the nature of your inquiry, it may take some time for me to respond.
  • Please do not send unnecessary personal information, such as your full name, home address, or phone number.
