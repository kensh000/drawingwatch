Privacy Policy

Kensho ("I", "me", or "the Developer") sets forth this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of user information in Drawing Watch (the "App"), an application provided by the Developer.

Article 1. Personal Information

In this Privacy Policy, "personal information" refers to information that identifies, or can reasonably be used to identify, an individual.

Article 2. Collection of Personal Information

The App does not collect users' personal information during normal use.

However, if you contact me with questions, suport requests, or other inquiries regarding the App, I may ask for your email address so that I can respond to you.

Article 3. Handling of Artwork and Related Data

Artwork created using the App and any related data are stored in he App's Documents directory on the device being used.

The App does not transmit this data outside the device unless you initiate an action that involves sharing, backing up, or transferring the data.

The App also transfers data in JSON format vetween a paired iPhone and Apple Watch when initiated by the user's actions.

Article 4. Sharing and Backup

When you use the App's sharing or backup features, your artwork and related data are sent to the destination you select.

When importing a backup file stored outside the App, the App impors the file you select.

These operations are performed based on your actions.The Developer does not access or retrieve data stored on your device or in cloud storage without your consent.

Please note that any third-party services you choose as sharing or backup destinations may handle your data according to their own privacy policies.The Developer is not responsible for how those services handle your data.

Article 5. Handling of Email Addresses Received Through Inquiries

Email addresses received through inquiries are used only to respond to your inquiries, provide support related to the App, and communicate with you in connection with these purposes.

Once the inquiry has been resolved and there is no longer a need to retain the email address, it will be deleted appropriately.

Article 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may revise this Privacy Policy when necessary.

Any revised version will take effect when it is published on this website.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me using the contact information provided on Contact .
